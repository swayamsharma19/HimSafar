ROHTANG PASS PERMIT RULES (2026)

Rohtang Pass is one of the most visited and most regulated attractions in Himachal Pradesh.

Route-specific permit rules:
- If you are using the Atal Tunnel to reach Lahaul, Keylong, or Ladakh, you do NOT need a Rohtang permit. However, e-Aagman registration is still required.
- If you are crossing Rohtang Pass itself to continue onward to Spiti or Ladakh, you need a separate "Beyond Rohtang Permit" — this is different from the standard tourism permit.

Common reasons tourism permits get rejected:
1. Expired PUC (Pollution Under Control) certificate — this is the most common rejection reason.
2. Vehicle age exceeding 10 years.
3. Incorrect or mismatched vehicle registration details submitted in the application.

Environmental restrictions (enforced by the National Green Tribunal, still active in 2026):
- No horse-riding at Rohtang, Solang Valley, or Marhi.
- No snow-biking at these locations.
- No paragliding at these locations.
- No snow scooters at these locations.
- Rohtang Pass is officially designated as a "Snow Experience Zone" rather than a general adventure activity area, which is why many adventure activities are restricted there specifically.

Recommendation: Always double-check permit details before submitting, since resubmission after rejection can cost travelers significant time during peak season.
