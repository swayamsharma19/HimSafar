Source: Himachal Pradesh Tourism Guides (seasonal travel guidance)
Topic: Best time to visit, weather by season, seasonal activities

OVERALL BEST TIME TO VISIT
Most guides recommend March to June (spring/summer) as the best general window, with pleasant weather and only occasional pre-monsoon thunderstorms. October to February is the winter/snowfall season and is best for snow-focused trips.

SUMMER (March to June)
Weather is pleasant across most of the state, making it a popular season, especially for places like McLeod Ganj and Dharamshala.
Good for: general sightseeing, trekking, paragliding, camping, water sports.
Note: Rohtang Pass and Keylong Pass remain frozen/snow-covered year-round regardless of season.

MONSOON (roughly late June to September)
City life and tourist attractions can be affected by landslides, erosion, and flooding.
Recommendation: visit at the very start of the monsoon (around June) to avoid the heaviest downpours later in the season; avoid high-altitude regions during peak monsoon.
Outdoor activities like trekking and camping may be limited or restricted for safety reasons; paragliding may be closed during heavy rainfall.
Always check current road conditions before planning a trip during this period.

WINTER (December to February)
Heavy snowfall is common, especially from late December through February; some areas see snowfall as early as March.
Temperature range: roughly -5°C to 10°C in higher altitudes, 5°C to 15°C in lower regions.
Good for: skiing, snowboarding, ice skating, winter festivals, snow sightseeing.
Popular winter destinations: Manali, Shimla, Kufri, Narkanda (snowfall); Dalhousie, Kasauli, Kullu, Solang Valley.
Caution: avoid high-altitude regions during peak winter months (December-January) unless acclimatized to freezing temperatures; heavy snowfall can cause road closures and disrupt travel, so plan and book accommodation in advance.

REGIONAL/SEASONAL HIGHLIGHTS BY MONTH (general guidance, not exhaustive)
- December: Manali, Shimla, and Kufri for snow vacations; Spiti Valley for the most extreme snow experience.
- March: McLeod Ganj, Palampur, and Dalhousie for light temperatures and smaller crowds.
- April-May: Tirthan Valley, Dharamshala, and Chamba for spring blooms and pleasant weather.
- June: Spiti, Kasol, and Kinnaur for arid, cooler landscapes as an escape from summer heat elsewhere.

CULTURAL EVENTS
Notable annual festivals include the Minjar festival, Phulaich, Rakhi celebrations, the La Darcha Fair (August), Lohri, the Halda Festival, and the Himachal Winter Carnival.

GENERAL TRAVEL NOTE
Himachal Pradesh has relatively poor rail connectivity in many areas, and some districts are less developed in terms of tourism infrastructure — road travel is the primary way to reach most destinations, so travel times can be longer than straight-line distance suggests.
