Topic: Inner Line Permit (ILP) and Protected Area Permit (PAP) rules

WHO NEEDS AN INNER LINE PERMIT

Foreign nationals require an Inner Line Permit (ILP) to visit certain protected inner areas bordering Tibet, specifically in:
- Kinnaur district
- Spiti area of the Lahaul-Spiti district

These permits are issued by:
- SDM (Sub-Divisional Magistrate), Manali
- SDM, Reckong Peo
- Deputy Commissioner, Lahaul-Spiti (based at Keylong)
- Deputy Commissioner, Kinnaur (based at Reckong Peo)

No foreigner may enter or stay in a protected area of Himachal Pradesh without a permit from the competent authority. However, foreign tourist groups of two or more people, sponsored by a recognized Indian travel agency with a pre-approved itinerary, may be permitted to visit specific listed locations for up to 30 days.

Restricted locations near the Tibet border in Kinnaur include (partial list): Sumra, Shalkhar, Chango, Nako, Maling, Yang Thang, Kaa, Leo, Hango, Pooh, Spillow, Moorang, Charang, Kunu, Lippa.

For foreign tourists specifically, an Inner Line Permit is required to visit: Khab, Samdo, Dhankar, Tabo, Gompa, Kaza, Morang, and Dubling in the Lahaul-Spiti area.

DOMESTIC TOURISTS

Domestic (Indian) tourists generally do not need a permit for most trekking in Himachal Pradesh. However, an Interline Permit is required for domestic tourists trekking in areas close to the China border.

For a small number of specific treks near the border, local police must be informed in advance, even for domestic tourists.

TREKKING AND ADVENTURE ACTIVITIES

Before undertaking adventure activities such as trekking, river rafting, or paragliding, tourists are advised to inform local authorities in advance — this includes the SDM, local police, and Tourist Offices — and obtain permission wherever required.
