Topic: General tourist safety guidelines and conduct rules

GENERAL SAFETY GUIDELINES

The Department of Tourism and Civil Aviation, Himachal Pradesh, issues standard safety guidelines for tourists visiting the state, coordinated with the Revenue Department's Disaster Management Cell and the Health and Family Welfare Department.

Key safety guidelines:
- Do not stand at the edge of a cliff or on boulders near riverbanks for photographs — this is a common cause of tourist accidents in hill areas.
- Before adventure activities (trekking, rafting, paragliding), inform local authorities such as the SDM, Police, or Tourist Offices in advance and take permission where required.
- Smoking is not allowed in public places, under the Prohibition of Smoking in Public Places Rules, 2008.
- Carrying any prohibited substance under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act, 1985, is a serious offense.
- Do not purchase articles made from rare or endangered species, or antiques older than 100 years — these may be restricted or require special permits to transport.
- Do not scribble on or disfigure rocks, trees, or buildings, including heritage structures.
- Keep cash and valuables secured; do not leave them unattended in hotel rooms; divide cash across different pockets/bags when traveling.
- Always keep an identity card or passport with you while traveling.

CULTURAL AND RELIGIOUS SITE ETIQUETTE

- Remove shoes before entering temples, mosques, tombs, dargahs, or gurudwaras.
- Cover your head with a cloth while inside a Gurudwara or Dargah.
- Observe local customs and traditions, especially at religious sites.

GENERAL ADVICE

- Do not accept food or drink from unknown fellow travelers on trains or road journeys, as a caution against tampering.
- Avoid being led into unplanned shopping or sightseeing detours by touts, unofficial guides, or unlicensed taxi drivers; rely on the Department of Tourism, HPTDC, or your hotel for guidance instead.
- Change currency only at authorized foreign exchange outlets, banks, or hotels, and always keep the receipt.
